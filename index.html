How We Monitored RDS Health Every Day

Since our backend used a lot of LINQ queries, checking AWS CloudWatch graphs for Amazon Aurora (RDS) was part of our daily routine. Whenever we saw a spike in CPU or memory, we checked which queries were running at that exact time, reproduced them on our local machine, and rewrote the LINQ query or offloaded heavy work to SQS.
